# Success criteria → where the app meets them

Each row lists the feature, where it lives in the app and in the code, and a quick test you can show in the IA video or use for Criterion E.

| # | Success criterion | How the app meets it | Screen · code | Test |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Record income and expenses with amount, date, description and category | Expense/Income toggle plus amount, date, category and description. Validated: amount > 0 with at most 2 decimals, real date, description 1–120 characters. Transactions can be edited and deleted. | **Add transaction** · `Screens.tsx → TransactionForm` | Save with an empty amount → error. Save `-5` → error. Save `1450 / today / Food / "Lunch"` → appears in the list. |
| 2 | Predefined categories (Food, Transport, Education, Personal Care, Entertainment, Miscellaneous) plus custom ones | All six are built in, plus Monthly Stipend and Other Income. "New category" adds custom expense or income categories (duplicate names rejected). Custom categories can be deleted, and their transactions move to Miscellaneous. | **Add transaction**, **Budgets** · `finance.ts → PREDEFINED_CATEGORIES`, `CategoryModal` | Add "Gym" → it shows in the dropdown and on Budgets. Add "food" → "already exists". |
| 3 | Create a monthly budget with limits per category | Each month has its own limits (switch months with the ‹ › control). "Copy last month" carries limits forward. | **Budgets** · `FinanceData.budgets[month][categoryId]` | Set Food = 13,000 for this month, go to next month → Food is empty there. |
| 4 | Automatically calculate remaining budget | Per category and in total: limit − spent this month. Recalculates on every change. | **Dashboard** "Remaining budget", **Budgets** · `finance.ts → budgetStatus`, `monthSummary` | Limit 5,000, spend 4,400 → "600 remaining". |
| 5 | Visual spending analysis (charts) | Donut chart of spending share by category, budget-vs-actual bars, cumulative spending-pace line vs budget, 6-month income/expense bars, largest expenses. | **Analytics** · `charts.tsx` | Add expenses in 3 categories → donut shows 3 segments whose percentages add to 100%. |
| 6 | Notify at ≥ 80 % of a category budget | The moment a category reaches 80%: a pop-up toast, an entry in the 🔔 alert centre (unread badge), a dashboard banner, and optionally a device notification. It notifies again when the category goes over 100%. Each alert fires once, and it resets if spending drops back below. | Bell icon, **Dashboard** · `finance.ts → budgetAlerts` (`ALERT_THRESHOLD = 0.8`), alert effect in `App.tsx` | Limit 10,000: spend 7,990 → no alert. +10 (exactly 80%) → "reached 80%". Spend past 10,000 → "over budget". |
| 7 | Estimate end-of-month savings from spending patterns and remaining budget | Projected spending = spent so far + an estimate for the rest of the month. That estimate blends your current daily pace with your usual monthly spending, weighted by how far through the month you are, so one large early payment doesn't skew it. Shows predicted savings, plus "savings if you keep to your budget" (income − max(spent, budget)). | **Dashboard** "Predicted savings" · `finance.ts → forecastMonth`, `projectRemaining` | Day 1 of a month with no history → no division by zero. Past month → shows actual savings. |
| 8 | Store transaction records and view previous financial data | Saved on the device (works offline) and backed up to the user's account. Switch to any previous month to see that month's dashboard, budget, charts and alerts. JSON backup export and restore are in Settings. | Month switcher, **History**, Settings · `hooks.ts → useFinanceStore` | Add data, reload the page or sign in on another device → data is still there. |
| 9 | Search / filter by category, date or keyword | Keyword search (matches description, category and amount), category, type, from/to dates, and quick ranges (this month, last month, last 3 months, all time). Shows totals for the results and can export them to CSV. | **History** · `finance.ts → filterTransactions` | Keyword "petrol" + "Last month" → only that month's petrol rows. |
| 10 | Summary dashboard: total expenditure, remaining budget, predicted savings | Four headline cards (total spent, remaining budget, predicted savings, savings goal %), plus alerts, category progress, recent transactions, spending pace and suggestions. | **Dashboard** | Open the app → all three figures are visible without scrolling. |
| 11 | Spending suggestions per category based on the savings goal | Works out the monthly saving your goals need (remaining ÷ months to deadline), then splits the rest of your income across categories. Weights are 70% your own last-3-month spending and 30% sensible defaults. A suggestion never exceeds what you normally spend: the difference becomes extra savings. Each category gets a personal tip ("Keep it under 300 PKR a day…"), and one click applies the suggested limits. | **Budgets** → "Suggested limits", **Dashboard** card · `finance.ts → suggestBudgets` | Set a goal of 25,000 in 4 months → the saving target is about 6,250/month and the limits shrink to match. A saving target above income → "more than your income" error. |
| 12 | Visual tracking of progress towards savings goals | A progress ring per goal, with saved/target, the amount needed per month to hit the deadline, and the projected completion month at your current rate (on schedule or behind). Multiple goals in priority order, plus a monthly net-savings bar chart. | **Goals** · `finance.ts → goalProgress`, `savedTowardsGoals` | Record income above expenses for a month → the ring percentage goes up. |

## Requested additions

| Request | Implementation |
|---|---|
| Live user counter in the corner, starting at 50 | Top-right of every screen, including sign-in. It reads `stats/public.userCount` from Firestore in real time (`onSnapshot`). Each new email adds 1 inside the same transaction that creates the user (`firebase.ts → registerUser`). Firestore rules allow only +1, once per account, and Firebase rejects a duplicate email. |
| Email verification on sign-up | `sendEmailVerification` emails a one-time link. The app is locked on a "Verify your email" screen until it's clicked, which it detects automatically. Resend has a cooldown. Firestore rules also refuse finance data for unverified accounts. |
| Admin only for `muhammadhadi.officialwork@gmail.com` | `isAdminUser()` requires that exact email and that it's verified. The nav item and screen aren't rendered for anyone else, and Firestore rules only let that verified email read other users' profiles. The admin portal lists the real registered users live: verification status, join date, last active, spending vs budget and goal %, with search, filter and CSV export. |
